§02 · Guest Teaching

Guest Lectures and Seminars

CIVE 361 · University of Nebraska-Lincoln · Fall 2026

Can We Close I-80 Tonight?

A lab on scheduling a work zone lane closure. Students act as the DOT engineer who decides when a contractor may close a lane on I-80. They use D/D/1 queueing to estimate the backup for each closure window, then check whether the back of the queue is visible to approaching drivers over a hill crest. Teams weigh safety risk against delay cost and recommend a closure window. The session used live polls and an interactive Excel model.

CIVE 990T · University of Nebraska-Lincoln · Fall 2026

Nobody Drives Like the Model Says

A Quantum-Inspired Representation of Driver Behavior

Guest presentation in the graduate Civil Engineering Seminar in Transportation Engineering. The talk showed why differences between drivers, and changes in each driver's behavior over time, matter for simulation, safety analysis, and automated vehicles, and how a quantum-inspired representation captures them from trajectory data.
