Teaching

Four courses across two universities, spanning programming, transportation engineering, surveying, and statics.

§01 · Courses

Courses Taught

University of Nebraska-Lincoln · 2024 to present

CIVE 202

Civil Engineering Analysis II

A project-based course teaching civil engineers to program in Python, so they can collect, organize, analyze, and visualize data, and automate engineering calculations. It pairs core concepts from mechanics, dynamics, and sustainable design with professional programming skills, so students learn to read a client's problem and build a working solution for it.

University of Nebraska-Lincoln · 2024 to present

CIVE 361

Principles of Transportation Engineering

An introduction to transportation engineering covering operator, user, and vehicle characteristics, transportation facility design, traffic operations and control, and transportation planning. Drawing on Mannering's Principles of Highway Engineering and Traffic Analysis alongside real trajectory and traffic datasets from ongoing research.

Jordan University of Science & Technology · 2012 to 2013

CE 343

Surveying Laboratory

A hands-on lab progressing from taping and chain surveying through levels, theodolites, tacheometers, EDM, and the total station, closing with field projects in traversing, contouring, and area measurement by planimeter.

Jordan University of Science & Technology · 2012 to 2013

CE 201

Engineering Statics

A foundational mechanics course spanning vector analysis, statics of particles, equivalent force systems on rigid bodies, equilibrium, supports and reactions, truss analysis by joints and sections, frames, shear and bending moment diagrams, and centroids and moments of inertia of composite areas.
